# Item Merchant replacement

Grounding revision `ImportedMerchantGroundedV3`: both exterior sacks and the amethyst cluster sit on the first entrance step; the gold-ore cluster sits fully inside the lower step. The crate pedestal is extended downward while preserving its top under the crates. `ground_merchant_props.py` applies the correction to both export scripts. `Merchant_GroundedProps.fbx` replaces only the five affected material meshes; the full `Merchant_Studio.fbx` also contains the corrected prop positions. `grounded_props_report.json` verifies zero support gap for all four prop groups. The builder adds two matching stone footings under the building and crate pedestal to close the map's remaining 0.6-stud ground gap. These footings bottom at world Y=0. There are eleven invisible collision boxes plus the two solid footings. Prior meshes and building are backed up under `ServerStorage.BeforeMerchantGrounding`.

Source: the supplied `D:/Models/Merchant/Merchant.blend` and eight PNG texture maps. `prepare_studio_import.py` exports `Merchant_Studio.fbx` with the same geometry and UVs, merged into 23 meshes by material. It excludes only the large presentation ground (`Ground_Cobblestone`). Source files remain unchanged. Material colors are restored from the Blender manifest after Studio import; the roof uses matte Slate with the original shingle texture.

`ReplicatedStorage/Shared/MerchantAsset.luau` records the uploaded mesh and texture IDs, original mesh dimensions, local transforms, colors, placement, and ten simple collision boxes. `MerchantModelBuilder` clones the original MeshPart templates from `ReplicatedStorage.MerchantAssets.MeshTemplates`, retaining the imported SurfaceAppearance. If templates are missing, it can reconstruct the same geometry and diffuse UVs with FileMesh parts from the asset IDs. The prepared FBX and texture copies are included for restoring native MeshPart templates through Studio's 3D importer.

Placement: pivot `(-50.2, 0.6, -112)`, facing +X toward Shovelkeeper, whose entrance faces -X. Both centers use Z=-112. The solid Merchant footprint ends at X=-30.5, outside the road border at X=-29. A narrow matching approach connects the stairs to that border. The render ground is not imported into the game.

The old active stall is removed. Its 34 root pieces and original imported model are backed up in Studio under `ServerStorage.BeforeMerchantReplacement`. The Shovelkeeper, central plaza, and road underlay are preserved.

Compatibility anchors remain at `Workspace.Plain.Merchant.ItemMerchant_Counter` and `ItemMerchant_Floor`, so existing merchant UI, sell proximity checks, bag upgrades, and tutorial targeting keep their established paths. The counter anchor is now `(-40.7, 5.6, -112)`. `InventoryService.EnsureMerchantWorldPrompts` ensures the replacement building before registering the existing E prompt.

Studio validation: the E prompt opens the existing Merchant UI; selling near the new counter reaches the inventory handler; the removed kiosk location returns `TooFarFromMerchant`. A separate default test profile sells three Pebbles for 30 coins and ends with an empty bag, without changing the player's inventory. The model is aligned opposite Shovelkeeper and its solid footprint does not overlap the road. The place has not been published live.
